## Glow Beauty | Marketing & Customer Analytics Dashboard

OVERVIEW

This project presents an end-to-end Power BI dashboard developed to analyze marketing performance and customer behavior for Glow Beauty.

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions including:
- Which marketing channels generate the highest return on investment?
- Which channels deliver the most valuable customers?
- How effective are paid versus owned channels?
- How loyal are customers after acquisition?
- What customer segments drive business performance?

--------------------------------------------------

DASHBOARD PAGES

1. Marketing Overview

Provides an executive summary of marketing performance, including:
- Total Revenue
- Total Spend
- Net Return
- ROAS
- CPA
- Paid vs Owned Channel Performance

2. Channel Performance Analysis

Evaluates channel effectiveness using:
- Revenue
- Spend
- ROAS
- CPA
- CPM
- CTR
- Conversion Rate

The objective is to identify the most efficient channels and highlight areas of budget waste.

3. Customer Analysis

Focuses on customer behavior and segmentation:
- Customer Demographics
- Active vs Inactive Customers
- Customer Purchase Frequency
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- High-Value Customers
- Revenue per Customer

--------------------------------------------------

KEY INSIGHTS

- The business generated $39.72M in revenue with an overall ROAS of 4.8x.
- Email was the strongest-performing marketing channel with a ROAS of 33.7x.
- Owned channels generated over 50% of total revenue while accounting for less than 10% of marketing spend.
- Customers aged 25-34 represented the most active purchasing segment.
- Approximately 77% of customers were inactive, indicating significant retention opportunities.
- The repeat customer rate reached 36%, reflecting moderate customer loyalty.
- Customer value varies considerably across acquisition channels.
